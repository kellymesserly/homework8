Romeo and Juliet Summary

By William Shakespeare

In Renaissance-era Verona, Italy, the noble families of the Montagues and Capulets are locked in a bitter feud, causing public brawls and unrest. Romeo Montague, initially lovesick over Rosaline, attends a Capulet masquerade with his cousin Benvolio, where he meets Juliet Capulet. However, conflict escalates when Tybalt, Juliet’s hot-headed cousin, kills Romeo’s friend Mercutio.
